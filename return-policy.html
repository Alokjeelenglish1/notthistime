Return Policy

Shop with confidence

Last updated: October 1, 2026

30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee: We want you to love every piece of jewellery from Aurelia & Co. If you are not completely satisfied, you may return your purchase within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

1. Return Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, your item must meet the following conditions:

  • Returned within 30 days of the delivery date.
  • In its original, unworn condition with no signs of wear or damage.
  • In the original packaging, including all jewellery boxes, pouches, and certificates.
  • Accompanied by the original receipt or proof of purchase.
  • Engraved or custom-made items are non-returnable unless they arrive defective.

2. How to Initiate a Return

To start a return, please follow these steps:

  • Email us at returns@aureliaco.com with your order number and the reason for the return.
  • We will send you a Return Authorization Number (RAN) and a prepaid shipping label within 48 hours.
  • Securely package the item with all original materials and attach the prepaid label to the outside of the package.
  • Drop off the package at your nearest carrier location.

3. Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will process your refund within 5 business days. The refund will be issued to your original payment method. Please note that your bank may take an additional 3-5 business days to post the refund to your account.

Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error or a defective product.

4. Exchanges

We offer free exchanges within 30 days of delivery. If you would like to exchange an item for a different size, color, or style:

  • Contact us at exchanges@aureliaco.com with your order number and the item you would like instead.
  • We will provide a prepaid return label for the original item.
  • Your new item will be shipped once we receive the original, or we can charge a temporary hold and ship immediately.

5. Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged or defective, please contact us within 7 days of delivery. We will arrange a free replacement or full refund, including return shipping costs. Please include photos of the damage in your email so we can resolve the issue quickly.

6. Lifetime Warranty

Every piece of jewellery from Aurelia & Co. comes with a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects. This covers:

  • Stone replacement for diamonds and gemstones that loosen or fall out under normal wear.
  • Repair of broken clasps, prongs, or chains due to manufacturing defects.
  • Re-plating of gold-plated items that fade under normal conditions.

The warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, accidents, improper care, or normal wear and tear. To file a warranty claim, email warranty@aureliaco.com with your order number and a description of the issue.

7. International Returns

For international orders, return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the item is defective or the return is due to our error. We recommend using a trackable shipping service for international returns, as we are not responsible for lost packages.

8. Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned or exchanged:

  • Custom-engraved or personalised jewellery.
  • Earrings (for hygiene reasons), unless sealed and unworn in original packaging.
  • Gift cards and digital vouchers.
  • Items marked as "Final Sale."

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our return policy, please don't hesitate to reach out:
